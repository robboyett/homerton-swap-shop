how it works

a free swap for homerton. books, toys and clothes the kids have grown out of, passed on to neighbours. nobody pays and nobody sells.

getting something

  1. 1find a book and tap "I'd like this". that reserves it for you, so nobody else can ask.
  2. 2message the owner on whatsapp to agree a time. it's up to you to get in touch.
  3. 3pick it up. say if plans change.
  4. 4tick "we've collected it" so it comes off the shelf.

giving something

  1. 1add books. scan the barcode and publish. a collection or series can go up as one post, with a photo and a rough count.
  2. 2someone reserves it and messages you on whatsapp.
  3. 3hand it over. if they go quiet, put it back on the shelf.

good to know

everything is free, always. no selling. your address is never on the site. you share it in whatsapp when you choose to. a reservation can be undone by you or by the owner at any time, and the book goes back on the shelf. invite only, for homerton.

stuck?

ask in the whatsapp group. rob looks after the site and will help.
